# Frienda Growth Audit

**Live → https://eddieatadapty.github.io/frienda-flow-rebuild/**

Five ideas to turn more Frienda users into subscribers, each built into a clickable version of Frienda's own onboarding. Prepared by Adapty.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Show who’s waiting before you ask | +10–20% CR, like to purchase |
| 02 | Make the paywall about her | +10–15% CR to purchase |
| 03 | Offer A: your yearly plan, first year half price | +10–15% ARPU, from more yearly plans |
| 04 | Offer B: a free trial, and test its length | +10–20% CR to trial or purchase · a test, not a sure win |
| 05 | Offer C: two plans instead of three | +5–15% ARPU, from a better plan mix |

Every price, rating and feature comes from Frienda's App Store listing, website or in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty’s expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across 30,000+ subscription apps, not measured lift for Frienda. They overlap, so they don’t add up.

Static single page, no build step: `python3 -m http.server`.
